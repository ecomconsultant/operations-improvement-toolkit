# Automation decision guide

**Automation is an option, not the objective.**

1. **Eliminate:** Is this step necessary or required?
2. **Simplify:** Can fields, approvals or handoffs be reduced?
3. **Standardize:** Are input, owner, output and exception rules clear?
4. **Assist:** Would templates, checklists or AI drafts help with human review?
5. **Automate:** Is the task repeatable, monitored and reversible when it fails?

## ROI discipline

Estimated net benefit per period = credible labor time avoided × fully loaded rate + validated rework costs avoided − maintenance costs − allocated setup costs.

Freed employee time is **not automatically cash savings**. Consider quality, privacy, reliability and implementation risk alongside costs.
