## Analyze

Distinguish symptoms from causes. Ask whether missing inputs, capacity, rules, handoffs or system limitations create the issue. Use the [root-cause worksheet](../templates/root-cause-worksheet.md). Label hypotheses as tested or untested.

## Improve

Consider eliminating, simplifying, clarifying, standardizing, assisting and automating, in that order. Compare candidates with the [priority matrix](../templates/improvement-priority-matrix.md).
