# Practical DMAIC

DMAIC = Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control. Scale the method to the size and risk of the organization.

| Phase | Question | Lightweight output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Define | What problem are we solving? | Scope, problem and owner |
| Measure | What is the current baseline? | Reproducible observations or “unknown” |
| Analyze | What evidence supports the cause? | Testable hypotheses |
| Improve | What small change can we trial safely? | Pilot, owner, risk check |
| Control | How will the gain last? | Procedure, metric, follow-up |

## Avoid

- Prescribing tools before defining the problem.
- Treating correlation as proof of cause.
- Claiming hours saved without a credible baseline.
- Automating unclear exception rules.
- Ending with recommendations but no accountable owner.

The goal is repeatable performance, not process diagrams for their own sake.
