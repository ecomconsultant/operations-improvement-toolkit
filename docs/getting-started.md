# Get started in 30–60 minutes

Choose **one** recurring process: order handoff, invoice approval, customer inquiries or inventory updates.

1. **Define:** Identify the trigger, process owner, customer and intended output.
2. **Measure:** Record a time, error or rework baseline with its measurement window.
3. **Map:** Document actual steps, tools, handoffs and exceptions.
4. **Analyze:** Find waiting, duplicate entry, unclear ownership or missing inputs.
5. **Prioritize:** Compare value, effort, risks and evidence before changing anything.
6. **Follow through:** Choose an owner, review date and objective success metric.

Use the [operational diagnostic](../frameworks/operational-diagnostic.md) to guide the process.

## Guardrails

Never invent missing metrics. Check with the people doing the work. Avoid putting private customer information into public repositories. For regulated or safety-critical operations, preserve applicable controls.
